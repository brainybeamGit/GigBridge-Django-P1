Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about billing, contracts, revisions, and milestone payments.

When a client hires a freelancer, the agreed milestone amount is deposited into platform escrow via Razorpay. The funds remain locked and protected while the freelancer works. Once the deliverable is completed, the client reviews the submission and clicks "Approve Work" to release payment directly to the freelancer.

Clients have full control. If the deliverable requires modifications, the client clicks Request Revision and provides detailed notes. The milestone automatically returns to "In Revision" status, and the freelancer is notified immediately to update the work before payment is authorized.
