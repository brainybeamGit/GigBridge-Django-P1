Frequently Asked Questions

Billing, contracts, revisions, and payments explained.

When a client hires a freelancer, the milestone amount is paid through Razorpay and held in escrow. The freelancer works while the money stays locked. After delivery, the client reviews the work and clicks Approve Work to release the payment.

The client clicks Request Revision and adds notes. The milestone moves to "In Revision", the freelancer is notified, and no payment is released until the client approves.

Private messaging opens once the client accepts a proposal. From then on, both sides can chat and share files inside the Messages workspace. Pending or rejected proposals do not have chat access.
