Frequently Asked Questions

View all FAQs

Quick answers to our most common client and freelancer marketplace questions:

When a client hires a freelancer, payment for the milestone is deposited securely into GigBridge's escrow before work begins. Funds remain safely in escrow and are only released to the freelancer once the client reviews and explicitly approves the submitted work.

As soon as a client marks a project milestone approved, escrowed funds are instantly credited to the freelancer's wallet balance. From there, withdrawal requests to bank accounts or UPI via Razorpay are processed typically within 24 to 48 business hours.

If deliverables do not match contract scope or communication breaks down, either party can open a ticket via this contact form or click "Raise Dispute" on the active contract view. Our resolution desk reviews milestones, chat history, and submitted code or assets to arbitrate fairly.

Verified clients can click "Post a Project" in the navigation bar, choose a category, specify required skills and deliverables, and set a fixed price or hourly budget. Qualified freelancers are alerted immediately and proposals typically start arriving within minutes.
